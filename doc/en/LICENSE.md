## Package and dependency boundaries

An installed public SDK package includes `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`; its manifest and SBOM
identify Apache-2.0. Third-party components retain their own licenses and notices, including
any applicable source-availability or relinking requirements. The SDK license does not
replace those terms. Inspect the selected package's dependency catalog and license files.

Separately supplied access-controlled extensions are outside this license. A package that
includes an extension also carries `LICENSE.extension` and records the combined license
expression. The Apache grant for the public SDK does not grant rights to the extension.
